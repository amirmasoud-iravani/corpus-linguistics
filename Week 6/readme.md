Welcome to Week 6 of Corpus Linguistics course. This week, we shift our focus from corpus construction and analysis to the practical application of corpora in language teaching, learning, and testing. We will explore two interconnected themes. First, we will examine how corpus evidence informs language assessment, vocabulary selection, and the construction of textbooks and dictionaries, with the goal of understanding how authentic data can shape decisions about what learners should know and how their proficiency is measured. Second, we will investigate corpus-informed teaching and Data-Driven Learning (DDL), where authentic usage examples and frequency data empower learners to discover grammatical, lexical, and phraseological patterns for themselves, moving beyond a reliance on textbook explanations alone. Together, these sessions demonstrate how corpus linguistics can bridge the gap between real-world language use and the language classroom.
