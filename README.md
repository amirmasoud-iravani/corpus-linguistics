Corpus Linguistics: Theory, Methods, and Applications

This repository explores the definition, core concepts, and theoretical foundations of corpus linguistics, then demonstrates how corpora are applied across digital humanities and broader society. Along the way, it covers standard methods for designing, compiling, constructing, and — where needed — annotating a corpus, illustrating how a corpus is built with a specific goal in mind. It also examines where corpus linguistics overlaps with computational linguistics and natural language processing, and in that light, shows how corpus linguistics remains valuable in the age of large language models.

Part 1 — Corpus Construction is the hands-on core of the repository, walking through the practical pipeline of building and analyzing a corpus in the order you would actually do it. It begins with data collection through web scraping and crawling, then moves to preparing the text using RegEx and text preprocessing techniques. From there, it covers annotation and tagging for richer analysis, followed by the management and storage of a constructed corpus, and finally text analytics. Each lesson builds toward a complete, purpose-driven corpus workflow.

The repository is intended for students, researchers, and practitioners who want both the theory behind corpus linguistics and the practical skills to apply it. Future sections will expand on theoretical foundations, real-world applications, and the relationship between corpus methods and modern NLP and LLM-based approaches.
